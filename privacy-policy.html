Privacy Policy — Idlix

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Idlix - Movies & TV ("we", "our", or "the app") is a movie and TV discovery and tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights available to you.

By installing or using Idlix, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally On Your Device

Idlix is designed with privacy as a priority. All your personal app activities are stored completely offline on your device:

  • Watchlist, Favorites, Custom Lists, and Reviews/Ratings: Stored locally in a secure database (Hive). This data is never sent to our servers.
  • User Preferences & Settings: Genre preferences, onboarding status, and app configuration are stored locally in your device's SharedPreferences.
  • Release Reminders: Movie release notifications are scheduled and processed locally on your device.

2.2 Data Collected Automatically by Third Parties

To provide movie data, app analytics, and ad-supported free access, the app integrates trusted third-party SDKs that automatically collect standard device information:

  • Device & App Information: Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, app version, language, and regional locale.
  • Advertising Identifier: Google Advertising ID (AAID) used by Google AdMob for fraud detection, frequency capping, and ad measurement.
  • Usage Analytics: Screen views, search keywords, movie detail impressions, and feature usage events (collected via Google Firebase Analytics).
  • Monetization Events: Ad impressions, ad format (banner, native, interstitial, rewarded), and estimated revenue metrics.
  • IP Address: Processed by third parties (Google, TMDB) to determine general coarse location (country/city level) for localized content and ads.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No user accounts, email registration, or social logins required.
  • No names, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No precise GPS location tracking.
  • No access to camera, microphone, photos, or device contacts.
  • No payment or financial information.

3. How We Use Information

  • To fetch and display real-time movie, TV, and cast information.
  • To deliver and optimize non-intrusive advertisements that keep the app free.
  • To analyze app performance, diagnose crashes, and improve navigation.
  • To remotely configure feature flags and manage ad pacing intervals via Firebase Remote Config.
  • To detect and prevent invalid clicks and fraudulent activity.

5. Advertising & Privacy Choices

Consent Management (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, the app presents a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can manage or revoke your consent choices anytime in Settings → Legal → Ad Privacy Options.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads

On Android devices, you can opt out of interest-based ads or reset your Advertising ID at any time by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete Advertising ID.

Rewarded Video Ads

Watching rewarded ads is strictly voluntary and user-initiated (e.g., to activate a temporary ad-free browsing session).

6. Children's Privacy

Idlix is a general audience application and is not intended for children under 13. The app explicitly enforces include_adult: false on all content queries. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your library data (watchlist, favorites, ratings, reminders) is stored locally on your device, it remains on your device until you clear the app cache from within Settings → Storage → Clear Cache or uninstall the application.

8. Data Security

  • All API communication is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.
  • Production release binaries are fully obfuscated to prevent tampering.
